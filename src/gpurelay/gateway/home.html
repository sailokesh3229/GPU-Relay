Finish long ML jobs on free GPUs

Free GPU sessions end after a few hours. GPU Relay saves your training or evaluation job before a session ends and continues it in the next free session, on Kaggle, Modal or Colab. The results are the same as one run without breaks. It uses your own accounts, and it never pays for GPU time unless you allow it.

How it works

Three steps. You do steps 1 and 2 once.

  1. Create your GPU Relay account
    • Choose a username, enter your email, and pick a password of 12 or more characters.
  2. Connect your accounts
    • After you sign up, the Set up page asks for the tokens listed below, one at a time.
    • Each token is checked with its platform before it is saved, then stored encrypted.
  3. Open your GPU Relay and submit a job
    • Upload a Python script (.py) or a notebook (.ipynb). GPU Relay first checks that it can continue the job after a break.
    • Most scripts need no changes. You can close the page: the job keeps running.

What to have ready

You need the first two. The others are optional.

  1. Hugging Facerequired
    • A free account on huggingface.co, and a token with write access (Settings → Access Tokens).
    • Why: your jobs and their saved progress are kept in a private dataset in your account.
  2. Kagglerequired for free GPUs
    • A free account on kaggle.com with a verified phone number. Kaggle only gives GPUs to verified accounts.
    • An API token (Settings → API → Create New Token).
    • Why: Kaggle gives about 30 free GPU hours each week, and GPU Relay starts the sessions for you.
  3. Modaloptional
    • An account on modal.com with a payment method added (Modal needs one before it starts GPUs), and an API token.
    • Why: $30 of free GPU use each month. GPU Relay never pays beyond that unless you raise your limit.
  4. Google Colaboptional
    • A Google account. In Colab, add a secret named GPURELAY_HF_TOKEN that holds your Hugging Face token.
    • Why: more free GPU time. When a job needs Colab, you open the notebook GPU Relay gives you and press Run all.
  5. Your paid GPU platformsoptional
    • Credit or an account on Google Cloud, AWS, Azure, RunPod, Vast.ai, Lambda or DigitalOcean, and its API key. At first sign-in GPU Relay asks which ones you have; you can add more later.
    • Why: after the free GPU time, GPU Relay uses the credit you bought, cheapest first, and never spends past it. Card spending stays $0 unless you raise it.
  6. Fix it with AIoptional
    • Claude Code (your Claude Pro or Max plan) or Codex (your ChatGPT plan) on your own computer. No API key needed.
    • Why: when a job stops with an error in its code, its page offers a fix you can apply and rerun with one click.

Your code and tokens stay yours

Your jobs run only in your own Kaggle, Modal or Colab sessions, never on this website's server. Everything GPU Relay creates in your accounts is private. Other users cannot see your jobs or your tokens.
